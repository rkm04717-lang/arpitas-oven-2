HOW IT STARTED

with a kitchen.

Arpita's Oven began with a simple idea: make something we'd be proud to put on our own table.

What started as a few experiments in the kitchen slowly became cakes for birthdays, celebrations, surprises and all the little moments that deserve something sweet.

We don't think a cake should just look good. It should feel like someone actually cared about making it.
